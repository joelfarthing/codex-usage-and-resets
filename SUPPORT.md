# Support

CUAR is an independently developed Filament Labs plugin.

For help, email <info@filamentlabs.io>. Include:

- CUAR version;
- operating system and architecture;
- Node.js version;
- sanitized `codex --version` output; and
- sanitized `node scripts/cuar.mjs doctor --json` output.

Do not send credentials, tokens, authentication files, raw App Server
responses, account identifiers, usage percentages, reset counts, reset or
expiration timestamps, opaque reset-credit identifiers, or reset-ledger
contents.

CUAR does not provide billing support or control OpenAI account limits. Account
and subscription questions belong with OpenAI support.
